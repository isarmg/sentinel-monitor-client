The Linux build uses FFmpeg under LGPL-3.0-or-later and includes OpenSSL

the operating system's TLS implementation. See the accompanying licenses
and dependency copyright notices for the complete terms.

Each platform release is accompanied by an xcoc-<platform>-relink-source.tar.gz

FFmpeg source and build configuration, native archives, and rebuild instructions.

license restricts reverse engineering for debugging modifications to LGPL
components. Read docs/media-worker.md and SOURCE-BUILD.txt in that archive.
